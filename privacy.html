Privacy Policy

Pirate Blitz

Last updated 2 August 2026

Overview

Pirate Blitz: Broadside (the “Game”, package com.blts.broadside) is a single-player mobile game published by BLTS Studios (“we”, “us”). We designed it to need as little of your information as possible. The Game does not require an account or sign-in, and it does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, or camera.

The Game does show advertisements to support development. To serve those ads, our advertising partners collect certain limited device and usage information, described below. This policy explains what is collected, by whom, why, and the choices you have.

Information the Game stores on your device

The Game saves your progress and settings so you can pick up where you left off — coin balance, unlocked levels, stars, high scores, selected character, upgrades, captain name, and audio preferences. This save data stays on your device. It is game data only, does not identify you personally, and is not sent to us.

Cloud backup (Google Drive)

The Game uses Android’s built-in Auto Backup. Android automatically backs your save data up to your own Google Drive and restores it if you reinstall or switch devices. This backup is provided and controlled by Google as part of Android, not by us; we cannot read it. It is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy, and you can manage or turn it off in your device’s Google / Backup settings.

Advertising

The Game displays rewarded advertisements (you choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward such as coins or a life). Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay, a mediation service operated by ironSource Ltd. (a Unity Technologies company), together with advertising networks such as Unity Ads and, where enabled, other partners integrated through that mediation (for example Google AdMob). These partners are independent controllers of the data they collect through their ad SDKs.

Information collected for advertising

To select, deliver, measure, and (where permitted) personalize ads, our advertising partners may collect and process:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID) and similar identifiers;
  • IP address and, derived from it, coarse (approximate) location such as country or region — not precise GPS location;
  • Device information such as model, operating-system version, language, network type, and screen characteristics;
  • Ad-interaction and app-usage data such as ads shown, viewed, or clicked, and basic diagnostics.

We (BLTS Studios) do not receive your advertising identifier or build advertising profiles ourselves; this data is collected by the advertising partners named above under their own privacy policies: Unity / Unity Ads, ironSource (LevelPlay), and Google (AdMob).

Your choices and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region that requires it, the Game presents a consent request the first time it runs, and only loads ads according to your choice. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from the Game’s in-app Privacy options, where available. Regardless of region, you can:

  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier, and opt out of ad personalization, in your device’s Settings → Google → Ads;
  • Choose non-personalized ads where the consent form offers that option.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CPRA). Because the advertising identifier is collected by our ad partners, requests about that data can be made to them via the policies linked above; you may also contact us at the email below and we will help direct your request.

In-app purchases

The Game may offer optional in-app purchases (for example premium character skins). These are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment card details; payment is handled by Google under its own terms and privacy policy.

Children’s privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Data retention and security

We do not hold personal data about you on our own servers. Data collected by advertising partners is retained and secured under their respective policies. Information transmitted by the ad SDKs is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information for money. The advertising identifier and related data described above are shared with our advertising partners so they can serve ads — which some privacy laws treat as “sharing” or a “sale” for targeted advertising. You can limit this through the consent form and your device’s ad settings as described under Your choices and consent.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new date and, where required, disclosed in the Game’s Google Play listing before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email blts.tech.ai@gmail.com.
