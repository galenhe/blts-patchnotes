Privacy Policy

Pirate Blitz

Last updated 10 August 2026

Overview

Pirate Blitz: Broadside (the “Game”, package com.blts.broadside) is a single-player mobile game. We designed it to need as little of your information as possible. You can play the entire Game without an account. You may optionally sign in with your Google account (via Google Play Games) to save your progress to the cloud, and the Game shows optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for an in-game reward. The Game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.

Information the Game stores

The Game saves your progress and settings so you can pick up where you left off. This includes things like your coin balance, unlocked levels, stars, high scores, selected character, upgrades, and audio preferences.

This save data is stored on your device (and, if you sign in with Google, backed up to your own Google account — see below). It is game data only — it does not identify you personally, and it is not used for advertising or tracking.

Signing in with Google (optional)

You can optionally sign in using Google Play Games. If you do, your Google Play Games player identity is used to save your progress to the cloud (Google Play Saved Games), so it carries across your devices, and to record game achievements. This is provided by Google Play Games Services and governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. Signing in is entirely optional — the whole Game is playable without it — and you can sign out at any time in the Game’s Settings. We use your Play Games identity only to sync your save and achievements; we do not receive your email address or contacts.

Cloud backup (Google Drive)

The Game uses Android’s built-in Auto Backup feature. Android automatically backs your save data up to your own Google Drive and restores it if you reinstall the Game or set up a new device, so your progress isn’t lost. This backup is provided and controlled by Google as part of the Android operating system, not by us; we cannot read it. It is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy, and you can manage or turn off backups in your device’s Google / Backup settings.

Advertising

The Game shows optional rewarded ads — short video ads you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward (such as an extra life). These are served through Unity LevelPlay and the advertising networks it mediates. To show and measure these ads, an advertising identifier on your device may be used and limited ad-request data may be shared with our advertising partners. We include no analytics services.

Before any ads are loaded, we ask for your consent using Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from the Privacy Settings option in the Game’s Settings. Our advertising partners’ use of data is governed by their own privacy policies, including Unity’s.

Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is consent-gated (see above) and served by our advertising partners under their own policies. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, or you have any questions, contact us at the address below.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your data. The data that leaves your device is limited to:

  • your game progress, backed up to your own Google account (Android Auto Backup, and Google Play Saved Games if you sign in);
  • an advertising identifier and limited ad-request data shared with our advertising partners to serve and measure rewarded ads, subject to your consent.

We do not share any other personal information with third parties.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date, and, where required, disclosed in the Game’s Google Play listing.

Contact
