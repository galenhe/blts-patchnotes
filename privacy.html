Privacy Policy

Pirate Blitz

Effective 28 July 2026

Overview

Pirate Blitz: Broadside (the “Game”, package com.blts.broadside) is a single-player mobile game. We designed it to need as little of your information as possible. The Game does not require an account, a sign-in, or any personal details to play, and it does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.

Information the Game stores

The Game saves your progress and settings so you can pick up where you left off. This includes things like your coin balance, unlocked levels, stars, high scores, selected character, upgrades, and audio preferences.

This save data is stored on your device. It is game data only — it does not identify you personally, and it is not sent to us or used for advertising or tracking.

Cloud backup (Google Drive)

The Game uses Android’s built-in Auto Backup feature. Android automatically backs your save data up to your own Google Drive and restores it if you reinstall the Game or set up a new device, so your progress isn’t lost. This backup is provided and controlled by Google as part of the Android operating system, not by us; we cannot read it. It is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy, and you can manage or turn off backups in your device’s Google / Backup settings.

Advertising and analytics

The Game currently shows no ads and includes no third-party advertising or analytics services. If this changes in a future update, we will update this policy and the Google Play “Data safety” details before that version is released, and any required consent will be requested in-app.

Children’s privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no personal data is gathered, there is nothing for a parent or guardian to request access to or deletion of. If you have questions, contact us at the address below.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your data and we do not share personal information with third parties. The only data leaving your device is the Android backup described above, which goes to your own Google account.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date, and, where required, disclosed in the Game’s Google Play listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email blts.tech.ai@gmail.com.
