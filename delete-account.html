Account & Data Deletion

Pirate Blitz

Last updated 12 August 2026

Overview

Pirate Blitz: Broadside (the “Game”, package com.blts.broadside) is a single-player mobile game. You can play the entire Game without an account. The only account the Game can use is an optional Google Play Games sign-in, which saves your progress to your own Google account. We do not operate our own servers or user database — we do not hold a profile about you. This page explains how to delete your game account and its associated data.

What data is associated with your account

The only data tied to a Google Play Games sign-in is your game progress (coin balance, unlocked levels, stars, high scores, selected character, upgrades and settings) and Play Games achievements. This is game data only — it does not identify you personally. It is stored on your device and, if you sign in, in your own Google account (Google Play Saved Games) and Android’s automatic backup to your Google Drive.

Delete your account and data yourself (fastest)

  1. Sign out in the Game. Open Settings in the Game and sign out of Google Play Games to disconnect it from your Google account.
  2. Delete your cloud save. Open the Google Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, and choose to delete the saved data for Pirate Blitz (or delete all Play Games data). This removes your cloud save and achievements for the Game held in your Google account.
  3. Delete on-device data. Uninstall the Game (or, in Android Settings → Apps → Pirate Blitz → Storage, choose Clear data) to remove the local save from your device.
  4. Remove the Google Drive backup. Android may keep an automatic backup of the Game’s data in your own Google Drive. You can delete it in your device’s Settings → Google → Backup (or in the Google One / Drive app under app backups).

Because this data lives in your own Google account and on your device, the steps above delete it directly and immediately — nothing about it is held on our side.

What is deleted, and what we keep

Following the steps above deletes your game progress, cloud save and achievements. We keep no personal account data ourselves, so there is nothing further for us to retain about you. Rewarded ads shown in the Game are served by third-party advertising partners under our Privacy Policy; any advertising data they hold is governed by their own policies, and you can change or withdraw ad consent at any time from Privacy Settings in the Game.
